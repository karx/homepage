## Godrej Woods: The Forest-Themed Urban Retreat
**Location:** Sector 43, Noida

Built around a private urban forest of nearly **600 trees**, this 10.95-acre site features a forest trail that winds through high-rise towers. It creates a community ecosystem that emphasises physical wellness and social connection, providing a buffer against city noise and pollution.

**Key Feature:** Nature-first philosophy with "Singapore-style" architecture.

---

## County 107: Vertical Bungalows
**Location:** Sector 107, Noida

A premier example of biophilic architecture in a high-rise format. Each apartment is designed as a "vertical bungalow," featuring expansive decks with integrated trees and automated irrigation systems.

**Key Feature:** IGBC Platinum Certification for high standards of energy and water efficiency.

---

## Experion Elements: Five-Element Concept Living
**Location:** Sector 45, Noida

This project focuses on a low-density layout specifically built around the five Vastu elements. Every apartment ensures scenic views and large balconies overlooking landscaped green spaces.

**Key Feature:** Low-density, elements-based planning for holistic spiritual and physical health.

---

## Renox Thrive: Modern Vastu Luxury
**Location:** Sector 10, Greater Noida

A project dedicated to **100% Vastu-compliant units**, using Mivan construction for high-end luxury and structural integrity.

**Key Feature:** Pure Vastu compliance for every unit.
