Hi, I'm Kartik — glad you found your way here.

I graduated in 2015 and started at **Microsoft (Bing)**, working on the Knowledge Graph Engine — building and curating the structured data layer behind search.

After that, I set up my own venture in Noida — [Akriya Technologies](https://akriya.co.in/), a product house in Web Technologies and IoT. We shipped a lot of things. Some of them even worked.

Since 2022, based in Bangalore. Currently at **PhonePe**, building **Data Platforms under Fraud & Risk Assessment** — Graph DB interfaces, data pipeline tooling, and AI tooling.

Builder, DIY enthusiast, occasional 3D printer, and a flexible-but-opinionated person.
