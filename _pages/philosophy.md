Himani Arora is built on a single conviction: the spaces we inhabit shape us as profoundly as our habits and diet. The modern city dweller faces an environment that is, by default, misaligned — magnetically, elementally, and botanically. We exist to correct that.

Our practice draws from three interlocking disciplines, each ancient in origin and validated by modern science.

---

## Vastu Shastra — The Science of Spatial Harmony

Vastu Shastra is not superstition. It is a 5,000-year-old architectural framework that maps human dwellings against the Earth's cardinal directions, magnetic fields, and elemental zones. Its prescriptions — where to sleep, where to place the kitchen, how to orient the entrance — have direct scientific correlates in circadian biology, magnetic orientation research, and environmental psychology.

We apply Vastu as a diagnostic tool for modern apartments, always through **non-renovation remedies** that work within fixed layouts.

## Pancha Mahabhutas — The Five Primordial Elements

At the root of both Vastu and Ayurveda sit the **Pancha Mahabhutas**: Prithvi (Earth), Jal (Water), Agni (Fire), Vayu (Air), and Akasha (Space). These are not metaphors — they are a classification system for the energetic properties of every zone in your home and every system in your body.

Balancing these elements spatially, botanically, and dietetically is the central work of Himani Arora.

## Naturopathy & Botanical Synergy

The body and the building are not separate systems. Naturopathy, as we practice it, is the integration of natural materials, botanical air purifiers, dietary rhythms, and elemental nutrition into your daily environment. We do not prescribe medicine; we design conditions for the body to self-regulate.

The plants in your home are not decoration — they are a second skin. We select them by Vastu direction, air purification property, and circadian influence.

## Biophilic Design — The 14 Patterns

Biophilic design is the application of humanity's deep, evolutionary affinity for nature to the built environment. Research across 40 years identifies 14 discrete patterns — from direct nature connections (views of greenery, the presence of water) to natural analogues (biomorphic forms, natural materials) — that reliably improve wellbeing, concentration, and stress resilience.

We integrate these patterns into every Vastu consultation as a practical, evidenced overlay.
